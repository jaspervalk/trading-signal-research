# Disclaimer

This project is research and educational software. It is **not** investment advice, an investment recommendation, or a solicitation to buy or sell any security.

The system attributes trade calls to named YouTube creators in order to evaluate the historical performance of those calls. Attribution is based on automated extraction from publicly available content; extractions may be incomplete or incorrect. Backtest results reflect historical price data only and do not guarantee future results. Costs, slippage, and execution feasibility are modelled with explicit, intentionally simple assumptions documented in `docs/decisions/0003-backtest-assumptions.md` — they are not realistic for live trading.

Creator scorecards and rankings are statistical summaries computed under the assumptions above. They are not endorsements, predictions, or assessments of any individual's professional capability.
